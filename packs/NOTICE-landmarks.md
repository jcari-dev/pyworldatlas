# PyWorldAtlas landmarks pack notices

The landmarks pack contains derived, transformed data from two sources:

- **Airports:** OurAirports (https://ourairports.com/data/), released to the
  public domain. Only airports with scheduled passenger service and an IATA
  code are included; elevations are converted from feet to metres.
- **World Heritage sites:** Wikidata statements (https://www.wikidata.org/),
  released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0: names, list numbers, locations,
  years of inscription, and selection criteria.

PyWorldAtlas is not affiliated with UNESCO or OurAirports and takes no position
on any boundary or status. Airport country codes are as published by
OurAirports; World Heritage sites carry coordinates only.

Locations are representative points and are not suitable for navigation. The
full source manifest, checksums, and transformation code are published in the
PyWorldAtlas repository: https://github.com/jcari-dev/pyworldatlas
