# PyWorldAtlas statistics pack notices

The statistics pack contains values from the World Bank's World Development
Indicators (https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/), licensed under Creative
Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Source: World Bank, World Development Indicators, snapshot of 2026-10-28
(last updated by the World Bank on 2026-10-08). Each indicator also names its
original source, such as the United Nations Population Division or the Food and
Agriculture Organization of the United Nations.

Changes: values are kept as published, rounded to the decimals shown, and
matched to PyWorldAtlas profiles by ISO 3166 alpha-3 code. Regional and income
groupings other than the world total are left out. Missing values are left
missing. PyWorldAtlas is not affiliated with or endorsed by the World Bank.

The full source manifest, checksums, and transformation code are published in
the PyWorldAtlas repository: https://github.com/jcari-dev/pyworldatlas
