# PyWorldAtlas map pack notices

Map packs contain derived, transformed data. They are offline visual aids and
are not suitable for navigation.

- **Elevation:** NOAA NCEI ETOPO 2022 60 arc-second ice-surface global relief
  model, sampled at 5 arc-minutes (maps) or 20 arc-minutes (maps-lite).
  Dedicated by NOAA under CC0 1.0. https://doi.org/10.25921/fd45-gt74
- **Outlines, rivers, and lakes:** generalized Natural Earth 1:10m and 1:50m
  vectors, in the public domain. https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
- **Climate classes:** Beck et al., 1991-2020 Köppen-Geiger map at 0.1 degrees,
  licensed under CC BY 4.0. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-023-02549-6
- **Renderer:** plotly.js 3.7.0 and the plotly.py "plotly_white" template, both
  MIT licensed. See `LICENSE-plotly.txt`.

The full source manifest, checksums, and transformation code are published in
the PyWorldAtlas repository: https://github.com/jcari-dev/pyworldatlas
